# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 17, 2026

Focus Button for YouTube does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal
information or browsing activity.

The extension stores one preference in the browser's local extension storage:
whether Focus mode is on or off. This preference remains on the user's device
and is used only to restore the selected mode.

The extension does not use analytics, advertising, remote code, accounts, or
external servers.

YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC. This extension is not affiliated with or
endorsed by Google or YouTube.
